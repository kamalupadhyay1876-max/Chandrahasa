# Chandrahasa: human voice & visual direction

## Direction
An editorial jewellery house, not a template storefront. Use the product as the protagonist. Warm paper, graphite ink, restrained brass accents, close crops, tactile materials and deliberate spacing. Motion should support the object rather than compete with it.

## Palette
- Warm ivory: `#F5F0E8`
- Ink: `#1A1A1A`
- Antique brass: `#B8860B` (accent only)
- Silver-grey: `#C0C0C0`
- Warm neutral and dark ink for body copy

## Typography and composition
Use a high-contrast serif for editorial headlines and a readable sans-serif for UI/body. Avoid excessive caps, repetitive cards, uniform icon tiles, gradient blobs, neon, generic sparkle motifs, false urgency, fake social proof and overly symmetrical sections. Allow asymmetry, large product photography, captions, negative space and short human-written copy.

## Authenticity rules
- No fabricated review text, customer names, workshop biographies, locations, years founded, staff identities, certifications, tax identifiers, addresses, response-time promises, sourcing claims or stock scarcity.
- No claim of BIS hallmark/HUID unless the particular item has a verified hallmark and the data is accurately associated with that SKU.
- No fake offer, coupon, shipping, returns, warranty or payment availability. Use the live database and policy pages.
- Images of craft processes are labelled illustrative unless the store owner confirms that they document the actual supplier/workshop.
- Store owner must replace all sample business identity and contact details before launch.
- Human review is required for product descriptions, translations, customer-facing policies and accessibility.
- Do not disguise authorship or provenance. Aim for distinctive, truthful brand design and maintain an honest record of how the project was made.

## Motion: 10 restrained behaviors
1. Scroll reveal (intersection observer)
2. Reveal variants (fade/scale)
3. Stagger timing for grouped content
4. Desktop product tilt
5. Hero pointer parallax
6. Image hover zoom
7. Hover lift/shadow
8. CTA shimmer (not on every button)
9. Scroll progress indicator
10. Optional floating/orbit/pulse accents, only where useful

All motion respects reduced-motion preferences; mobile pointer tilt is disabled. Keep animations short, calm, and avoid hiding content from keyboard users or screen readers.
