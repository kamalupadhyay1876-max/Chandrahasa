# Motion system

10 effects/classes included, with no additional library:
1. Scroll reveal fade/slide
2. Scroll reveal directional (left/right)
3. Scale reveal
4. Staggered children reveal
5. Scroll progress bar
6. Pointer-based card tilt (desktop only)
7. Floating animation (`.motion-float`)
8. Shimmer hover (`.motion-shimmer`)
9. Image zoom hover (`.motion-image-zoom`)
10. Glow lift hover (`.motion-glow`), plus orbit and pulse utility classes

Use classes on elements as needed. Animations automatically respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch/mobile devices don't receive pointer tilt.
