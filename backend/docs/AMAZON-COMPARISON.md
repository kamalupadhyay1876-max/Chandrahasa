# Focused store vs Amazon — realistic comparison

Chandrahasa is a single-store commerce starter, not a marketplace or logistics platform. It has customer-facing catalogue/cart/account/order workflows and operator tools, while Amazon operates marketplace seller systems, vast search/recommendation infrastructure, fraud/risk engines, fulfillment network, distributed inventory, extensive accessibility and reliability programs, review moderation at scale, pricing, tax, international logistics and support infrastructure.

## 25 capabilities to consider as product roadmap
1. Product variant/SKU inventory model
2. Reservation TTL and worker
3. Stock ledger and adjustment reasons
4. Purchase-order/restock workflow
5. Low-stock threshold per SKU
6. Barcode/SKU labels
7. Inventory export/import with validation
8. Order timeline and operator notes
9. Shipment retry/idempotency
10. Split shipment support
11. Return merchandise authorization (RMA)
12. Refund approval workflow and ledger
13. Exchange workflow
14. Shipping label/manifest integration
15. Delivery exceptions and customer notification
16. Transactional email/SMS templates and delivery logs
17. Gift cards/store credit
18. Loyalty points
19. Product bundles
20. Wishlist share links
21. Verified-purchase reviews/moderation
22. Search facets and merchandising
23. Analytics funnels and cohort retention
24. Admin roles, least privilege and MFA
25. Backup/restore, uptime monitoring and incident response

Prioritize reliability, accurate product data and support—not cosmetic imitation of Amazon. Roadmap items are not implemented claims.
