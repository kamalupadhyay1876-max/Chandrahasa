# Chandrahasa — Client sales guide

## 30-second pitch
Chandrahasa is a premium, mobile-first jewellery commerce experience built around product storytelling and operational control. Customers can discover products, manage a cart, check out, and follow their orders; store operators have a central area for catalog, order, customer, coupon and fulfilment workflows. The design is meant to be lightweight and easy to run, while payment, shipping, security and database services are configured to the client's actual business and verified before launch.

## One-minute pitch
Rather than handing you a static website, Chandrahasa gives you a commerce workflow: catalogue management, product information, checkout, customer accounts, order operations, stock management, discount codes, fulfilment tracking and recovery tools. The package includes a deployment guide and a clear release gate. We tailor brand content, domain, policies, payment/shipping providers, analytics and staff access, then run a real acceptance test before switching customer payments on.

## Discovery questions before quoting
- What is your catalogue size, SKU/variant/size model and inventory source?
- What payment methods, COD rules and refund policy do you need?
- Which shipping partner, pickup address, service zones and return flow?
- Who owns product photos, copy, hallmark/material data and pricing?
- GST registration, invoicing, HSN and tax treatment?
- Domain, email sender, hosting, privacy policy, terms and returns policy?
- Staff roles, MFA, auditability and approval rules?
- Expected traffic, backup/recovery target and support SLA?

## Demonstration sequence
1. Browse collections and inspect a product.
2. Add product to cart and show cart controls.
3. Show checkout with payment configuration clearly in test mode.
4. Demonstrate order lookup/tracking with a test order.
5. Show admin catalogue and inventory editing.
6. Show order operations, coupon management and fulfilment fields.
7. Explain what is live, what is test-mode and what is custom work.

## Suggested commercial packages (illustrative; price after scope)
- **Launch setup:** brand/site configuration, catalogue import, policies, domain/hosting configuration and acceptance checklist.
- **Commerce setup:** payment/shipping sandbox configuration, customer email flows, inventory workflow and staff training.
- **Care plan:** updates, backups monitoring, monthly health review, content changes and support SLA.
- **Custom scope:** migrations, analytics, loyalty, advanced ERP, integrations, high-availability, custom tax/invoicing and security assessments.

## Honest objections
- “Is it the same as Amazon?” — No. It covers a focused small-business commerce workflow, not Amazon's global infrastructure, logistics, marketplace, fraud systems, staffing or scale.
- “Can you launch today?” — A demo can run locally. A live store requires client credentials, product/legal content, provider tests, hosting, domain and acceptance sign-off.
- “Are payments live?” — They should remain in test mode until sandbox tests and production configuration are verified.

## Proposal template
Deliverables: configured storefront/admin, catalogue onboarding, deployment, provider configuration in sandbox, training, test report, launch checklist and agreed support. Exclusions: provider fees, legal/tax advice, third-party service outages, custom integrations not scoped, ongoing content unless contracted. Acceptance: agreed browser/device checks, order and refund/return scenarios, backup/restore, provider sandbox checks and sign-off.
