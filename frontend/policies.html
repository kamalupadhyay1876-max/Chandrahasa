Refund timelines and pickup eligibility are confirmed during the return approval process. Custom/engraved pieces and gift cards are non-returnable. Free size exchange within 30 days.

Shipping & Delivery

Free shipping above ₹999 (below: ₹79), insured and tracked. Metro cities: 2–4 business days. Rest of India: 4–7 business days. If an order arrives damaged, contact customer support promptly with clear photographs so the team can review the request.

Silver Care

Sterling silver loves skin contact and dislikes sulphates (perfume, chlorine, rubber). Wipe with the included cloth after wear; store in the zip pouch; keep away from direct perfumes and sanitizers. Dullness is natural oxidation, not damage — the polish cloth restores the mirror. Avoid ultrasonic cleaners for oxidised/antique finishes.

Privacy Policy (DPDP Act Compliant)

What we collect: contact details, shipping address, order history, and (if you create an account) credentials stored only as scrypt hashes. Why: fulfil orders, communicate delivery updates, honour returns and warranties. What we never do: sell your data, store card numbers (payments are Razorpay PCI-DSS L1), or track you across the web. Your rights: under the DPDP Act you may request access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal at any time — use the contact page to submit a privacy request. The final data-retention period, hosting region and response SLA must be confirmed in the published privacy notice.

Terms of Service

Prices in INR, inclusive of GST. Orders are confirmed on payment authorisation; stock is allocated first-come-first-served — the odd piece may sell out between add-to-cart and checkout (we’ll always tell you immediately). Ownership transfers on delivery. HUIDs are authentic government-registered hallmarks; any mismatch, report within 15 days. The seller identity, registered address and governing jurisdiction must be confirmed on the final tax invoice and order terms.

Cookie Policy

We use exactly one functional cookie: ch_session (httpOnly, SameSite=Lax, 7 days) to keep you signed in. No advertising trackers, no fingerprinting. Analytics, if ever added, will be aggregate and consent-gated. Clear it any time from your browser; you’ll simply be signed out.

Authenticity & Hallmark Guarantee

For products advertised as hallmarked, check the hallmark and HUID information supplied with that individual item. A provided HUID can be checked on the BIS Care portal. Contact support if the product details and verification result do not match.
